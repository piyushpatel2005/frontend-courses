A workshop notice was updated twice: Tuesday is no longer the meeting day, and an edited draft changes what to bring. Run the completed page to see two crossed-out phrases with different meanings.

`<s>` marks information that is no longer accurate or relevant, without claiming it was an edit to a document. `<del>` means text was removed from a revision; `<ins>` means new text was added. Browsers often strike through both `<s>` and `<del>`, but their meanings differ. Use these tags for meaning, not merely a decorative line.
