A science corner has a formula, an area unit, and a note marker. Run the finished page and compare the small lowered digit in water with the raised digits in the other two lines.

`<sub>` lowers a character that belongs below the baseline; `<sup>` raises one above it. Keep the surrounding word or formula outside the tag: `H<sub>2</sub>O`, not `<sub>H2O</sub>`. A superscript marker is not a substitute for linking a real footnote in a longer document.
