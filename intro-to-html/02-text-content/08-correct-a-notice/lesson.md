The previous notice showed an outdated day separately from a tracked draft change. The starter has the old and new text in plain paragraphs. Add only the wrappers that explain each relationship.

## Your Tasks

1. In the first paragraph, mark only `Tuesday` as no longer current with `<s>`; keep `Wednesday` visible.
2. In the second paragraph, mark `Bring two tools.` as removed with `<del>`.
3. In the second paragraph, mark `Bring one tool.` as added with `<ins>`.

Run to inspect the two kinds of strike-through; Submit to check the actual elements, not just their appearance.
