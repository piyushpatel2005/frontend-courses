Your poster from the previous lesson now has a warm page background, a dark title, and readable details. This starter already includes those finished rules. Do not start over or remove the colors: give the white poster a visible edge, then soften it.

The border draws the edge. `border-radius` rounds its corners; `box-shadow` adds depth without changing the layout or replacing the border. Work on the existing `.poster` rule in `style.css`.

## Your Tasks

1. Keep the previous step's warm background, dark heading, and spaced details; add `border: 2px solid #537c6d` to `.poster`.
2. Add `border-radius: 12px` to `.poster`.
3. Add `box-shadow: 0 8px 18px rgb(24 54 42 / 15%)` to `.poster`.

Run after each declaration. First you should see a green edge, then rounded corners, then a subtle shadow below the card. Submit to check the three steps. The poster is now ready as a simple visual design; the next module explains the box layers that give it its spacing.
