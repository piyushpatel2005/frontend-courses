A neighborhood repair table needs a simple poster visitors can read at a glance. The HTML is supplied; this first project step changes only the page background, heading text, and the spacing between lines of details. No layout trick is needed yet.

The starter already limits the poster's width and gives it a white surface. Keep those rules. Add the three declarations in the marked rules of `style.css`.

## Your Tasks

1. Give `body` a warm `background-color: #f7f4ed` so the white poster stands out.
2. Give `h1` a dark `color: #17324d` so the event title is easy to read.
3. Give `.details` a `line-height: 1.6` so its two lines have more room.

Run after each change and compare the preview: first the page behind the poster changes, then the heading, then the details. Submit when those three small changes are in place. In the next lesson you will keep this same poster and give its surface a clear edge.
